EVERY STEP DESERVES RECOGNITION

Celebrating our students.

At SSBA, we recognise effort, celebrate progress and encourage every student to keep growing.

Achievement means more than winning a match. Taking part, learning a new skill, improving technique and supporting others all deserve recognition. Our student recognition celebrates these milestones and helps build confidence, motivation and pride in every player’s badminton journey.

Every player’s journey is different. We encourage students to take pride in their own progress and celebrate one another’s achievements.

What should I bring?

Non-marking indoor badminton shoes with socks, a badminton racket, comfortable sportswear, a water bottle and a towel. Shoelaces must be securely tied.

Can a complete beginner join?

Yes. Junior coaching includes a beginner pathway, and beginners are welcome in women’s coaching. Adult social sessions suit intermediate and advanced players.

How do I enrol and find out the fees?

Call or email us, or prepare an enquiry below. Tell us which program you’re interested in and the player’s experience level. We’ll confirm availability, fees and the next steps.

Which junior coaching level is right for me?

Beginners learn the basics, intermediate players refine techniques and movement, and advanced juniors develop competitive strategies and match readiness. Ask Kiran about the best fit.
